## Strict LIVE cycle boundary

The live parent uses 518,400 canonical input bytes and 560,640 accepted bytes including semantic HUD data, plus 1,025 hash jobs per profile. Strict last-required-byte-to-validated-commit durations are 3,062,014 cycles (fast) and 5,732,576 cycles (stalled); both exceed the 1,250,000-cycle under-10-ms budget at an assumed 125 MHz. First-byte-to-commit numbers (3,092,122 and 5,772,781) are different measurements and must not replace the strict last-byte interval. These are modeled cycles, not achieved device clocks. LIVE uses abstract memory/publication; ESP32 execution, physical SDRAM, full validated decode under 10 ms, and hardware 30 fps are false/unqualified.
