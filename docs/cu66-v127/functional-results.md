Those original attempt bytes were preserved; their initial fail-closed `FAIL` fields are not interpreted as a functional failure or a pass. Parent execution followed both terminal passes and an explicit [Root dispatch grant](../../experiments/python-gamebub-v127/orchestration/parent-dispatch-grant.json).

All executable Verilator builds retained assertions, `-Wall` and strict C++ warnings; the V97 generated library remained library-only with a separately strict adapter. Heavy jobs used at most two study slots, each two CPUs, compiler job one, 8 GiB RAM and 8 GiB total RAM plus swap, finite watchdogs and the frozen RAM/pressure gates. Fresh successful stage host durations were 510.68 seconds peer, 1,050.30 seconds decoder/transport and 579.61 seconds parent; these are orchestration durations, not FPGA decode latency.

This qualification establishes functional identity for the retained fixtures. It does not establish routed component timing, complete-parent timing, a board bitstream, physical memory behavior, calibrated ESP32 Wi-Fi/QSPI/CPU cost, a complete custom Sunshine endpoint, sustained distinct gameplay frames at 30 fps, or validated hardware decoding under 10 ms.
