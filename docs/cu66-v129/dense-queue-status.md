# V129 dense-owner queue: independent evidence review

**Disposition: standalone architectural experiment; do not integrate or claim timing closure yet.** The [RTL](../../fpga/experiments/cu66-v129-dense-queue/v129_dense_owned_queue.sv) and [testbench](../../fpga/experiments/cu66-v129-dense-queue/tb_dense.cpp) implement and check a two-slot queue with its own explicit handshake contract. They do not establish equivalence to the existing producer/scratch boundary.

The [source-bound receipt](../../experiments/python-gamebub-v129/dense-queue/attempts/20261111T001353099008Z/receipt.json) records actual Verilator builds and executions with assertions and strict warnings. Both independent-seed runs passed 60,020 cycles each. The bench checks full tuple order and compares ready, valid, ownership, occupancy and cursor against a cycle model; directed and random cases cover full and stalled offers, simultaneous push/pop, block wrap, reset/kill, and the supplied drain fences. The [input freeze](../../experiments/python-gamebub-v129/dense-queue/focused-prelaunch-freeze.json) matches the receipt's source hashes; the receipt and its recorded artifact hashes were rechecked after execution. The randomized traffic and scoreboard are finite tests, not formal proof.

The mutation evidence was actually executed: the receipt records separate successful builds and exit code 1 for each mutated binary. The [truncated-child-epoch run](../../experiments/python-gamebub-v129/dense-queue/attempts/20261111T001353099008Z/truncate_child_epoch-129501.log) reports a control mismatch, and the [unsafe-full-accept run](../../experiments/python-gamebub-v129/dense-queue/attempts/20261111T001353099008Z/unsafe_full_accept-129501.log) reports a ready/valid mismatch. These two controls demonstrate sensitivity to those specific faults only.

## Unverified integration obligations

- **Reset and cancellation:** reset and kill synchronously invalidate queue control, while payload registers are deliberately not reset. The bench checks logical flushing, including randomized resets and kills; it does not prove system-wide reset sequencing, asynchronous crossings, or the treatment of transactions already accepted outside this queue. No retained payload may regain ownership without a valid new arm.
- **Physical memory debt:** `memory_debt_empty` and `child_quiescent` are external Boolean assumptions. The test drives them and checks gating, but models no actual physical requests, acknowledgements, outstanding counts, mismatched acknowledgements, or post-cancel drain. The surrounding design must preserve and drain every accepted physical write and corresponding ACK before retry or finish, including same-epoch retry.
- **Producer and publication contract:** a wrong-owner offer can handshake before the fault latches; a previously valid head can simultaneously retire. The producer and parent must account for both events, preserve fault/error priority and never publish the bad tuple. `publication_context_ready` is supplied externally and the bench varies it without modeling actual MAP_REQ/MAP_WAIT metadata. The parent must fence metadata reuse, block/plane transitions and the 16-bit acquisition-block wrap. Full-queue pop has a recovery bubble; the producer must tolerate it and the resulting changed latency.
- **System performance:** no real producer adapter, complete frame/hash oracle, physical timing run, board test, sustained 30 fps test or under-10-ms decode measurement is included in this unit result. Integration requires a latency-aware end-to-end regression with cancellation, faults and outstanding ACKs, followed by a fresh constrained route and performance measurements. See the [prototype contract](../../fpga/experiments/cu66-v129-dense-queue/README.md).
