# Custom codec prototype checkpoint V58 — 2026-11-05

V51–V58 add transport-state recovery, a YUV scanout interface, several bounded motion/quality studies, and a stricter 16×16-cell zeroing probe. Across these experiments, none establishes device throughput, physical 30 fps, or FPGA/ESP32 resource fit. The experiment folders and retained verifiers are indexed by `docs/prototype-v58-provenance.json`.

## Evidence by question

- **Transport recovery (V51):** 804 actions across 24 cases produced 800 receiver calls: 588 accepted, 212 rejected, four dropped. The API-level harness demonstrates recovery in its software receiver state. It does not measure network behavior, panel recovery, or physical reset/retransmission.
- **YUV scanout (V52):** 96 actual frames cover 46,080 lines, 33,177,600 RGB565 output words, and 2,027,520 HUD words. The readonly YUV reference model uses two chroma rows per plane and emits one RGB565 line; it does not retain a full RGB frame. Its nominal model charges both paths for HUD and compressed-stage traffic and predicts a 45.3888 MB/s delta under the stated 720×480, 30-decode/60-scanout bus assumptions. This is conditional traffic arithmetic, not measured bandwidth, panel timing, BRAM fit, or an exclusive custom-codec benefit; another YUV decoder could use the same scanout path.
- **Quantized motion (V53):** on four 24-frame histories, selected complete bytes fall by 0.313–2.185%, while active source MSE rises by 0.282–3.504%. The local guard's choices are not a quality-preserving Pareto win: future references produce higher cumulative error than separate fixed-zero histories. The 69 native prefix points in the comparison are reused V40/V41 measurements, not new encodes. Across all 16 compared policy/cohort cases, best sampled HEVC points use 31.67–68.23% fewer bytes and AV1 points 38.48–75.85% fewer bytes than custom at the matched mean-MSE gate. These are reused native prefixes, not fresh encodes; the mean gate does not imply peak/every-frame equivalence or a universal codec ranking.
- **Motion receive path (V54):** 198 actual receives across nine histories include 148 frames with nonzero vectors and 10,650 nonzero vector instances. Luma and chroma each contain 1,725 fractional-vector instances. Six directed fractional fixtures retain 36 full/compact alternatives. The tiled receiver exactly matches the direct receiver's coefficients, vectors, canonical references, HUD, and display, but averages 227.405 ms versus 72.776 ms for direct receive in this Python scope. V54 is dataset/receiver qualification, not a new algorithmic speed result.
- **Exact motion eligibility (V56):** the 96-frame audit finds zero eligible nonzero macroblocks after post-RDO checks. No mixed motion was selected; the experiment therefore has no eligible motion saving to report.
- **Error diagnostics (V57):** 480 decoded presentations across 20 policy/native-anchor cases reuse 96 source frames. Coordinate-wise temporal error-change energy is a diagnostic of error at fixed coordinates, not perceptual flicker or motion-compensated quality. Lower values do not prove reduced perceived flicker.
- **Exact 16×16-cell probe (V58):** 96 causal selected frames retain the actual zero32 packet and reference. The probe marks 110 visible cells as eligible and 1,669 hidden-only cells, but all 110 visible cells have unchanged post-RDO coefficients and equal fine-bit counts; none has fewer bits. Zero-motion remains selected throughout, so there is no packet-byte gain. The proxy never reruns quantization/RDO, and its exact causal replay is bounded to the archived prefixes.

The combined V52, V53, V56, V57, and V58 test logs contain 42 passing tests. V51 and V54 are dataset qualifications that inherit earlier codec tests and add no new test count. V53's 3 producer + 6 independent tests, V56's 4 + 6, and V58's 4 + 8 are independently replayed; V52 has 4 model + 3 memory-budget tests, and V57 has four metric tests.

## Scope limits and reproduction

The source clips are related Mass Effect walkthrough material, not evidence of broad game coverage. Native comparisons reuse frozen points and are not fresh encodes. Host timing excludes the complete capture/encode/transport/decode/display chain unless a study explicitly says otherwise. No inference from isolated component times establishes 30 fps. No study here demonstrates FPGA cycles, routed clock, ESP32 transport capacity, BRAM/RAM fit, or deployed panel behavior.

The provenance exporter freezes the V50 parent except the two maintained indexes, inventories only V51, V52, V53, V54, V56, V57, and V58, and stores the source-pinned results and replay commands. V55 and all later studies are excluded.
