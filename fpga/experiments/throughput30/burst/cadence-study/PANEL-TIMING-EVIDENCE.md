# Rev4 hypothetical 27.0709 MHz display clock: evidence

**Conclusion:** the model's ~60 Hz timing is within the published *ILI9806E controller* clock and frame-rate ranges and meets the cited controller horizontal blanking minimum. The exact physical Rev4 LCD module's specification and actual board behavior remain unknown, so this is not confirmation that the complete panel/board supports the timing. No hardware was driven or changed.

## Identification and sources

* Local upstream checkout `/home/lori/gamebub` is commit `ce607713c7dcd87fe42d5c0de5d4be040e61a47f`, branch `sd-bridge-v1`, origin `https://github.com/elipsitz/gamebub.git`. Its [Rev4 selection](https://github.com/elipsitz/gamebub/blob/ce607713c7dcd87fe42d5c0de5d4be040e61a47f/fpga/src/main/scala/platform/handheld/HandheldTop.scala#L74-L87) configures the `ILI9806E` driver, 480×800 rotated scanout and horizontal offset -28; [driver source](https://github.com/elipsitz/gamebub/blob/ce607713c7dcd87fe42d5c0de5d4be040e61a47f/fpga/src/main/scala/platform/handheld/display/ILI9806E.scala#L7-L57) describes a nominal 60 Hz clock range of 25.8–35.0 MHz. The [firmware driver](https://github.com/elipsitz/gamebub/blob/ce607713c7dcd87fe42d5c0de5d4be040e61a47f/firmware/handheld/src/device/drivers/ili9806e.rs#L44-L64) configures ILI9806E RGB DE mode, active-high HS/VS/DE, and data capture on the falling edge. These identify the intended controller in source; they do not prove the exact component fitted to any particular physical device.
* [ILI Technology ILI9806E controller datasheet, page 48, section 3.4.2](https://www.crystalfontz.com/controllers/Ilitek/ILI9806E/472#page=48) is an original manufacturer document hosted by a distributor, not an official Ilitek-hosted download. For the indicated DPI RGB timing it lists DCLK **16.6–41.7 MHz**, frame rate **54–66 Hz**, H sync at least 1 clock, H back porch 2–126 clocks, H front porch at least 2 clocks, V sync and back porch 1–126 lines, V front porch 1–255 lines, and total horizontal inactive time at least **2 µs**. The figure expressly states particular control-polarity settings; firmware programs different polarities, so this table comparison alone does not verify every electrical/setup/hold condition of the configured interface.
* [Official Game Bub FAQ](https://docs.gamebub.net/faq/) describes the horizontal screen as a **720×480** 24-bit IPS LCD with variable refresh. The checked source models **480×800** portrait scanout with a landscape offset, and the study presents a 720×480 canvas within that raster. The physical visible region and panel-specific model/maximum ratings were not independently verified; do not silently treat these descriptions as an identified module datasheet. No separate display bridge part or its data sheet was established from the inspected source.

## Compared model timing

The isolated `README.md` cadence experiment changes only the testbench pixel half-period to 18.470 ns: DCLK = 27.070915 MHz. `../../../../rtl/moonshine_lcd.sv` uses horizontal total 541, active 480, active start 14; vertical total 834, active 800, active start 24; both sync pulses last 4 clocks/lines. With the testbench's waveform definitions this gives H sync/back/front **4/10/47 clocks**, V sync/back/front **4/20/10 lines**, and refresh `27,070,915 / (541 × 834)` ≈ **59.998 Hz**. Horizontal inactive time is **61 / 27.070915 MHz ≈ 2.253 µs**. These modeled values are numerically within the controller table's cited DCLK, refresh and porch ranges, and within the upstream source's 25.8–35 MHz 60 Hz target range. This comparison assumes the waveform's pulse/porch conventions correspond to the datasheet for the programmed polarities; board-level waveforms have not been measured.

The simulation used a synthetic clock, not an implemented MMCM solution or physical display. Exact module-qualified DCLK/refresh, full signal setup/hold and jitter margins, physical clock generation and timing closure, image quality, power and hardware operation are not established. No statement here establishes sustainable QSPI/network throughput or 30 fps hardware performance.
