# Simulation provenance and limits

The 35 product RTL files are byte-identical to source freeze58be226c and the routed FAIL candidate f40f32c1. `sim-inputs` copies the executed ea400273 full01 inputs without source substitutions. The extra platform TB is byte-identical to the saved platform01 input; its new standalone replay deliberately omits SIMULATION to exercise real controller IO branches. Support primitives and bounded SDRAM are repository-authored functional simulation models, not AMD/Xilinx vendor libraries. They omit analog delay, jitter and metastability. The original GameBub CERN-OHL-S license and all source notices are preserved. No third-party native C++ source or compiled vendor models are included. Golden column/composite assets and fixed ROM data are bound independent comparison data; they are never used to initialize candidate SDRAM.

The exact native C++ oracle provenance and full original artifact corpus remain bound by the included ea400273 manifest and selection hashes; this package includes its executed input/output subset. A full fresh replay is available, but no new full-run result is claimed merely by packaging identical inputs. The retained original full01 completed 691200 exact pixels/two canvases,40 actual QSPI snapshot words,139 checks and actual CRC4c5725e5. Repeated canvases of one fixture are not unique FPS. No routed timing, hardware, external QSPI hold or warm memory-retention qualification follows.
